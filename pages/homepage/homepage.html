Your Custom Jewelry Journey in Los Angeles

Most people walk into a jewelry store and choose from what's behind the glass. At A. D'Mae Diamonds, nothing exists until you walk through the door.

Alexandria is a GIA Graduate Gemologist and third-generation jeweler working from a private studio in West Los Angeles. Every project starts the same way: a one-on-one conversation about who you are, who you're designing for, and what this piece needs to say. No inventory. No sales team. No pressure. Just a jeweler who listens first and picks up a pencil second.

From custom engagement rings to wedding bands, heirloom jewelry redesigns to anniversary pieces, every design is built from scratch around your story. Ali hand-selects every stone, walks you through the tradeoffs honestly, and keeps you involved at every step - from initial sketch to CAD rendering to the final reveal.

Whether you're planning a proposal, honoring a family tradition with an inherited stone, or creating something bold that's never existed before, the process is the same: personal, transparent, and built entirely around you. Clients visit in person at our West Los Angeles studio or work with Ali virtually from anywhere in the country. Wondering what custom jewelry actually costs? We break that down honestly. And our pricing and budget guide walks you through how we handle the money conversation.

Your consultation is free, takes about 90 minutes, and comes with zero obligation. Most clients say the same thing afterwards: "I wish I'd come here first."
