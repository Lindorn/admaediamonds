How It Works

Every ring starts the same way: with a conversation. Here's what the journey looks like from first meeting to final reveal.

1

We Get to Know You

Your free 90-minute consultation isn't a sales pitch. It's a conversation. We learn about your relationship, your style, your priorities, and what this ring means to you. In person in our Los Angeles studio or virtually from anywhere.

2

Sketches & Design

Ali drafts initial sketches based on your vision. You give feedback, we refine, and we keep going until it feels exactly right. Most clients nail it in the first round.

3

Stone Selection

Ali hand-selects stones tailored to your design and budget. Natural diamonds, lab-grown diamonds, sapphires, emeralds, or whatever your heart is set on. Every option is GIA Graduate Gemologist or independently verified. No mystery sourcing.

4

CAD Rendering & Production

You'll see a photorealistic 3D render of your ring before it's ever made. Once you approve, your ring goes into production with our trusted artisan partners. Precision casting, hand-setting, polishing, and finishing.

5

The Big Reveal

Your finished ring arrives with a professional appraisal, a presentation box, and care instructions. Most custom engagement rings are completed in 6-8 weeks from your first sketch. Need a custom wedding band to go with it? Many couples design both in the same process.

Common Questions About Custom Engagement Rings

How much does a custom engagement ring cost in Los Angeles?

Every ring is designed from scratch, so pricing depends on your stone choice, metal, and design complexity. Most of our engagement rings fall between $3,000 and $20,000+, though we've created beautiful pieces at every price point. Our pricing page walks through every factor, and our cost guide covers real budget ranges. Your initial consultation is free, and we'll provide a detailed estimate after we understand your vision.

How long does it take to make a custom engagement ring?

Most custom engagement rings take 6-8 weeks from your first sketch to the finished piece. If you're working on a tighter timeline (say, a surprise proposal date), let us know upfront. We can often accommodate rush timelines depending on design complexity and stone availability.

Do you work with lab-grown diamonds?

Absolutely. We work with both natural and lab-grown diamonds, as well as a wide range of colored gemstones. Ali will walk you through the differences in quality, value, and sourcing so you can make the right choice for your priorities and budget. We don't push one over the other. We educate, and you decide.

Can I use my own diamond or a family heirloom stone?

Yes. Repurposing heirloom stones is one of our specialties - learn more about our heirloom redesign process. Ali will evaluate your stone for quality, durability, and design compatibility. If it passes her standards, we'll design a new setting that gives it a second life. This can also significantly reduce cost since you're not purchasing a new center stone.

Do I need an appointment, or do you have a showroom?

We work by appointment only from our private studio in West Los Angeles. This means your consultation is completely private — no crowds, no distractions, no sales floor. We also offer virtual consultations for clients outside of LA. Every meeting is one-on-one with Alexandria.

What makes A. D'Mae different from the jewelry stores in the LA Diamond District?

Most jewelers in the Diamond District sell from existing inventory and work on commission. We don't carry inventory and we don't have a sales team. When you work with A. D'Mae, you're working directly with a GIA Graduate Gemologist who designs your piece from scratch. Every stone is hand-selected for your specific project, and every design decision is made together. It's a fundamentally different experience - one Ali built after leaving the corporate jewelry world. Personal, unhurried, and built around you.
