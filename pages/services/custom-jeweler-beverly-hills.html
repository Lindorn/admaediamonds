What Beverly Hills Stores Can't Offer

When you buy from a retail jewelry store, part of what you're paying for is their overhead. The lease, the display cases, the inventory they're carrying, and the commissions their sales team earns. Those costs are built into the price of every piece they sell.

With Ali, every dollar goes into your stone, your metal, and your craftsmanship. There's no showroom to maintain and no inventory to finance. That's how custom engagement rings start at $3,000 while delivering a level of personalization and attention that a retail store structurally cannot match.

Ali also searches the entire global market for your stone - not one supplier's current inventory. That means you're seeing the best options available anywhere, not just what a store happens to have behind the counter this week. Here's how that sourcing process actually works.

How is this different from a Beverly Hills jewelry store?

Retail stores sell from inventory. Ali designs from scratch. There are no display cases and no premade rings. You work directly with a GIA Graduate Gemologist who handles every step. The result is a piece that was made for one person - you.

Is custom more expensive than buying from a store?

Often comparable or less. Custom engagement rings start at $3,000. Retail stores build their overhead into every price tag - rent, inventory, commissions. With Ali, every dollar goes into what you're actually getting: the stone, the metal, and the craftsmanship. Full pricing breakdown here.

Absolutely. Ali sources from the global market and has access to rare cuts, exceptional quality stones, and colored gemstones at every price point. Whether you're looking for a large, investment-grade natural diamond or a rare Colombian emerald, she can find it.
