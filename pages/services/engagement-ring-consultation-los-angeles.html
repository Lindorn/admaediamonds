Questions About the Consultation

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. The 90-minute design consultation is completely free with no strings attached. If you choose to move forward with a custom piece, a $500 deposit starts the design process. That deposit is applied toward your final price. If you decide not to move forward, you owe nothing.

Can I bring my partner to the consultation?

Of course. Many couples come together to design their ring as a shared experience. If you're planning a surprise proposal and want to come alone, that works too. Ali has helped plenty of clients keep the secret.

What if I have no idea what I want?

That's one of the most common starting points and it's totally fine. Ali will ask you questions about your partner's style, your lifestyle, what metals and colors they gravitate toward, and what feels meaningful to you as a couple. By the end of the conversation, you'll have a direction. That's the whole purpose of the meeting.

How do I book a virtual consultation?

Same booking process. When you schedule your appointment, just let us know you'd like to meet virtually. We'll send you a link for FaceTime, Zoom, or Google Meet. The virtual experience is the same full 90-minute consultation, and Ali can share her screen to walk you through design references, diamond options, and portfolio examples in real time.

Do I need to have a budget in mind before the consultation?

It helps, but it's not required. If you have a range in mind, Ali can tailor the conversation to options that fit. If you're not sure, she can walk you through what different budgets typically look like so you can find a range that feels comfortable. Either way, there's no minimum spend and no judgment.
