Budget is one of the first things we talk about. Not because we want to sell you something - because we want you to relax.

So we get it out of the way early. Tell Ali your budget - whatever it is - and she'll work within it. No judgment, no upselling, no "well, for just a little more..."

If your budget is firm

If your budget is flexible

That's fine too. Ali can walk you through what different ranges typically look like so you can find what feels comfortable.

What Affects Your Price

Five factors shape every quote. We walk you through each one so there are no surprises.

Stone Type

The biggest factor - typically 50-70% of the total cost. Type, carat weight, cut, clarity, and color all play a role. Lab grown diamonds, natural diamonds, and colored gemstones each have completely different economics. We walk you through every option honestly. Lab grown vs natural is part of this conversation.

Metal Choice

14k gold, 18k gold, platinum, or mixed metals. Each has different cost, durability, and aesthetic properties. Precious metal prices fluctuate constantly - sometimes daily - which is another reason we can't post fixed prices.

Design Complexity

A clean solitaire costs less in labor than a hand-engraved multi-stone setting. Simple doesn't mean boring, and complex doesn't have to be unaffordable. We find the sweet spot together. See how the design process works.

Your Existing Assets

Have an heirloom stone or old gold jewelry? Repurposing can reduce the total cost by 40-60%. It's one of the smartest moves in custom jewelry. Learn about our heirloom redesign process.

Timeline

Standard turnaround is 6-8 weeks from sketch to finished piece. Rush options are available when possible and may impact pricing. We always discuss timeline and cost together upfront.

Common Questions About Pricing

We know budget conversations can feel uncomfortable. These are the questions clients ask most.

How much does the consultation cost?

Nothing. It's completely free - 90 minutes, no obligation. If you choose to move forward, a $500 design deposit starts the sketching process. That deposit is applied toward your final price.

Can you give me a ballpark before we meet?

For general ranges, our blog post breaks down what different budgets look like in practice. For something specific to your vision, the consultation is the fastest path to a real number. You can also call or text Ali directly if you just want a quick gut-check before booking.

Can you work with my budget?

Yes. Ali is genuinely excellent at maximizing value within constraints. Tell her your real number and she'll show you what's possible. No judgment. Financing through PayPal Credit (6 months interest-free) and Pay in 4 are also available if that helps you get closer to the piece you actually want.

When do I pay?

$500 design deposit to start. Then 50% before production begins - this is when we secure your center stone. Final 50% before pickup or delivery. No surprises.

Why can't you just post prices for each piece?

Three reasons. First, every piece is different - even small changes in stone quality, band width, or metal type can dramatically shift the price. Second, precious metal and stone markets change constantly, sometimes daily. And third, we're protective of our clients' privacy. We don't think the price someone paid for their ring should be public information.
