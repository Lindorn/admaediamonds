An Alternative to the Jewelry District

If you're in Santa Monica and starting to think about an engagement ring or a custom piece, you've probably considered driving downtown to the jewelry district. It seems like the obvious move - hundreds of vendors, wholesale prices, endless options.

The reality is different. The district is overwhelming. You're navigating competing vendors on your own, evaluating stones without gemological training, and trying to figure out who to trust in an environment built around volume and negotiation. It can feel like buying a car.

Working with Ali is the opposite experience. You have a personal GIA Graduate Gemologist who handles everything - design, sourcing, production oversight, and delivery. She searches the entire global market for your stone, not one supplier's inventory. And the consultation is 90 minutes of focused, unhurried time. Here's what that actually looks like.

Absolutely. Ali has worked with multiple clients who planned proposals at Santa Monica restaurants, beach spots, and nearby landmarks. She'll design around your timeline and have the ring ready for pickup at the studio before your proposal date.

Most custom engagement rings take 4-8 weeks from first consultation to delivery. If you're working on a tighter timeline, Ali can often accommodate that. The process is structured around you, not a factory schedule.

Yes. Ali designs custom necklaces, earrings, bracelets, anniversary pieces, and statement jewelry. If you have a vision for something personal, she can bring it to life. See the portfolio for examples.
