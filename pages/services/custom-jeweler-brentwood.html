More Than Engagement Rings

Most people find a custom jeweler when they're looking for an engagement ring. But the relationship doesn't have to stop there, and the work doesn't have to start there either.

Ali designs custom necklaces, earrings, bracelets, and statement pieces alongside engagement rings and wedding bands. Anniversary gifts. Birthday presents. Self-purchases for people who just want something beautiful that was made specifically for them. If you have a vision for a piece of jewelry - or even just a feeling about what you want but no clear direction yet - that's more than enough to start a conversation.

She also specializes in heirloom redesign. If you have family jewelry sitting in a drawer - your grandmother's ring, your mother's earrings, stones from a piece you don't wear anymore - Ali can transform them into something new that you'll wear every day. Using inherited stones can cut the total cost of a custom piece by 40-60%.

Most projects involve 2-4 visits: an initial consultation, a stone approval, a design check-in, and a final pickup.
