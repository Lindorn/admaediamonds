Questions About Working With a Local Custom Jeweler

Where exactly is your studio?

Ali's studio is located on Barrington Ave in West Los Angeles (90025), close to Brentwood and Westwood. All consultations are by appointment only, ensuring a private and relaxed experience. There is convenient street parking available.

Do I need to come in person?

No. Ali works with clients both in person and virtually. Many couples in the LA area prefer the in-person experience, but the virtual consultation process is equally thorough. Several of Ali's best projects have been designed entirely remotely.

How is this different from going to a jeweler in the DTLA jewelry district?

The jewelry district is great if you know exactly what you want and you're comfortable evaluating stones and settings on your own. Ali's approach is different: she sits down with you, learns about you, and handles the entire process from design through sourcing through production. You're not navigating a wholesale market alone. You have a personal designer and gemologist guiding every step.

What does a consultation cost?

Consultations are always free, and there's no obligation to move forward. Ali wants you to feel comfortable and confident before committing to anything. Most consultations run about 90 minutes.

How much does custom jewelry cost?

Custom engagement rings start at $3,000 with no ceiling. The cost depends on your stone selection, metal choice, and design complexity. Ali is transparent about pricing throughout the process and will give you a clear estimate after your consultation. You can learn more on our pricing page or read how much a custom engagement ring actually costs.
