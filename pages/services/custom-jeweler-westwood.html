Questions From Westwood Clients

How far is the studio from Westwood and UCLA?

About a 5-minute drive. Ali's studio is on Barrington Ave in West LA (90025), just south of Wilshire. It's one of the closest custom jewelers to the Westwood and UCLA area.

I'm on a tighter budget. Is custom realistic for me?

Yes. Custom engagement rings start at $3,000, and some of the most interesting pieces Ali has ever designed came from tight budgets that required creative problem-solving. Constraints don't limit the work - they shape it. The consultation is free and there's no commitment.

How is this different from going to the jewelry district downtown?

In the jewelry district, you're navigating a wholesale market on your own - comparing vendors, evaluating stones without expertise, and hoping you're not getting overcharged. With Ali, you have a personal GIA Graduate Gemologist guiding the entire process. She handles design, sourcing, production, and delivery. You don't need to be a diamond expert. That's her job.

Is parking available?

Yes. Free street parking on Barrington Ave and surrounding streets. The studio is appointment-only, so there's never walk-in traffic competing for spots.

Can I bring my partner, or should I come alone?

Either way works. Some clients come alone for the surprise factor, some bring their partner because they want to design together. Ali adjusts the consultation to fit your situation. There's no "right" way to do this.
