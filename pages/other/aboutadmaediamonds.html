Meet Alexandria

Your guide through the custom jewelry experience. Not a salesperson - a GIA Graduate Gemologist, a third-generation jeweler, and someone who genuinely cares about getting this right.

My grandfather opened a jewelry store in Iowa after becoming a watchmaker through the GI Bill after World War II. He built Necker's Jewelers from scratch, side by side with my grandmother. Their legacy inspired my dad, and now me.

For as long as I can remember, jewelry has fascinated me. The blending of ancient stones, billions of years old, with deep human experiences. An engagement ring, a graduation signet, an heirloom from someone we love - these aren't just objects. They're our stories and our legacy.

Yet I've always disliked the typical jewelry shopping experience. Pushy sales floors, impersonal interactions, and corporate priorities that leave people feeling unheard and pressured into buying whatever is in stock. It never felt right. I realized that as an industry, we were losing trust - and instead of addressing it, we were digging in deeper.

I founded A. D'Mae Diamonds to rebuild that trust. (If you want the full story, including why I left Brilliant Earth, I wrote about it on the blog.)

My Commitment to You

What Sets Ali Apart

World-Class Stone Sourcing

Most jewelers source from a handful of preferred wholesalers and call it a day. Ali doesn't. She searches the entire global supply - every database, every network, every avenue available - to find the best quality stone at the best price for your specific project.

She's exhaustively thorough. She cross-references certifications, catches deceptive listings that other jewelers would miss, and personally evaluates every stone before it ever reaches a client. Her natural diamond pricing consistently beats competitors including major online retailers, because she's not limited to one supplier's markup.

This isn't just a skill. It's the thing clients mention most in their reviews.
